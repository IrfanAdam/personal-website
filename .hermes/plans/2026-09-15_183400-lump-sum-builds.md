# Lump-sum builds — shipments with no scoped plan

> **For Hermes:** the standing catch-all. A shipment no scoped plan covers appends one `### Task N: …` under the phase it belongs to (or opens a new phase) and closes with `*Shipped in <sha> · Tasks a–b · phase-N.*`.

**Goal:** Nothing ships untraceable — every push lands on a scoped plan or here, with its sha recorded next to what changed.

**Architecture:** One running plan file; each phase batches related shipments and each task records what changed plus the sha that proves it, so `git log`, `.hermes/plan-links.json` and `/ds/#/changelog` agree with each other.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS + Vite, portfolio surface only (`main.js`, `src/views`, `src/styles`, `index.html`). Work inside `src/ds` gets a scoped plan of its own.

**Tags:** Layout, Motion
