# Lump-sum builds — week of Sep 21, 2026

> **For Hermes:** the weekly catch-all — one file per calendar week (Mon–Sun). A shipment no scoped plan covers appends `### Task N: …` under the phase it belongs to (or opens a new phase) and closes with `*Shipped in <sha> · Tasks a–b · phase-N.*`. At rollover the closing week is sealed and the next week opens its own file — a lump-sum plan never carries into a new week.

**Goal:** Nothing ships untraceable — every ad-hoc shipment lands in the week it shipped, with its sha recorded next to what changed.

**Architecture:** One file per week, phases numbered from 1 inside it; each phase clubs the week's related shipments and each task records what changed plus the sha that proves it, so `git log`, `.hermes/plan-links.json` and `/ds/#/changelog` agree. Week runs Mon 00:00 → Sun 23:59 local; a shipment opened before the seal finishes in its opening week's file.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS + Vite, portfolio surface only (`main.js`, `src/views`, `src/styles`, `index.html`). Work inside `src/ds` gets a scoped plan of its own.
