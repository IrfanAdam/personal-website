# Glitch lab split — narrower card, wrapping sound panel

**Goal:** The Glitch lab reads as a narrow demo card left with a sound panel right that wraps inside its own width instead of clipping at the panel edge.
**Architecture:** CSS-only change in `src/ds/ds-lab.css` — the `.fx-split` grid gives the card track less share and a zero minimum, the panel track a zero minimum so it can shrink, and every panel child gets wrap rules (`min-width: 0`, `overflow-wrap`, select/input fill).
**Tech Stack:** Vanilla CSS, token-only (`var(--size-*)`, `var(--space-*)`), no new modules.
**Tags:** Design System, Layout
